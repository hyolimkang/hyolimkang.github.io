I am an Assistant Professor in the [Department of Infectious Disease Epidemiology and Dynamics](https://www.tm.nagasaki-u.ac.jp/nekken/en/departments/epidemi-dynamics.html), [Institute of Tropical Medicine](https://www.tm.nagasaki-u.ac.jp/nekken/en/), and [School of Tropical Medicine and Global Health](https://www.tmgh.nagasaki-u.ac.jp/en/) at [Nagasaki University](https://en.nagasaki-u.ac.jp/).

My research focuses on **climate-sensitive arboviral disease dynamics**. I use mathematical modelling to understand outbreaks and generate evidence to guide the prioritisation and deployment of medical countermeasures for epidemic preparedness and response.

## Kang Research Group

The Kang Research Group develops quantitative approaches to understand, predict, and respond to emerging and re-emerging infectious disease threats, with a particular focus on climate-sensitive arboviral diseases. By integrating epidemiological, serological, and clinical data with statistical inference, spatial analysis, and mathematical modelling, we generate evidence to support outbreak preparedness, response, and public health decision-making.
