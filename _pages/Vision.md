## Vision
To advance equitable epidemic preparedness.

## Mission
To generate scientific evidence on outbreak dynamics, disease burden, and prevention strategies to inform public health decisions on neglected and emerging infectious diseases.

## Research Culture

We aim to build an inclusive, collaborative, and supportive research environment where people from different backgrounds and career stages can contribute ideas, ask questions, and develop as independent researchers.

Our group values **rigorous and reproducible research**, **open and constructive discussion**, **mutual respect**, and **equitable collaboration**. We encourage transparent methods, thoughtful sharing of data and code where appropriate, and recognition of individual contributions. Through regular research discussions, mentorship, and interdisciplinary partnerships, we seek to create opportunities for learning, professional development, and research that addresses public health needs.
